This is something that happened to me years ago, when I was probably 25.

I was working at a bar as a server and after my shift I had my shift drink. The bartender made it pretty strong and I was pretty tipsy. I actually might have had two, I don’t really remember, so when my bus arrived I just ran for it. I didn’t stop to think about going to the bathroom before leaving. I don’t know how long it had been since I’d gone, either, it could have been a few hours before the end of my shift. I had to pee, but it wasn’t too bad, and I was pretty sure I could make it the whole 40-ish minutes to my apartment, including the walk.

It started getting worse and I was getting nervous, but I was still kind of tipsy so I wasn’t really too worried. It wasn’t until I was maybe five stops and fifteen to twenty minutes from where I could get off the bus that I really started to feel the pressure. I had to sit up straight and still and not really let myself get distracted. My bladder felt pretty rock solid at this point as all the alcohol was rushing into it on top of whatever was left over from my shift.

I was trying so hard not to let anyone see how desperate I was to pee, I was staring straight into the void, willing the time to move by but every bump and jolt of the bus was a reminder that I was sitting on a mountain of pee, my teeth floating in the back on my head, struggling like crazy to hold it together. I think I even sat right across from the door so i could bolt immediately when I needed to. I was absolutely dying and dreading my presence on this bus. My face was getting hot, and I was starting to sweat.

By the time we got to my stop, my body was so determined to hold it in that I was shaking. I could barely keep from dancing at the bus stop and kept pacing around waiting for the light to turn green so I could cross the street to my apartment complex. With trembling fingers, I pulled out my keys as I speed-walked towards the gate, lest I have to stand there for too long. I remember my fingers and toes actually tingling a little bit as the desperation sent waves through my body. My bladder was pulsing now, and I just kept moving, my resolve not to have a public accident driving my every move. I don’t have any idea how I kept my hands out from between my legs. I think sitting on the bus had formed a solid seal, but the dam was crumbling quickly and I needed to get to my apartment, praying my roommate was not there to see me either more desperate than I had been in my life, or having wet myself.

The walk up the three flights of stairs was torture - move too fast and I’d break the seal, and move too slow and I’d run out of time. I was almost completely sober by this point, and felt every inch of my stretched and agonized bladder. Finally, the last flight of stairs I had to shove my hand between my legs and hold on for dear life while I unlocked the door, praying no one would see and pressing against the wave that I’d have to fake against if my roommate was home. I fumbled getting the keys in and was dancing on my spot, dreading having to pull it together as I opened the door.

Hesitantly I called his name while straightening up for a second, and when I saw the dark apartment and didn’t hear a response I threw down my bag, threw my hands back between my legs and slammed the door - I could lock it after. I danced and scuttled to my bathroom and alternated getting my pants off and holding myself between hands, my knees alternating bending as the latchkey effect hit me full force. I was careful about getting on buses without peeing first in the future because oh, my God I would have died of embarrassment. However, it is definitely a fun memory to go back to.
