There are levels of write off in the UK.

The lower levels the insurance company feels it's not worth their time to repair the car, these cars can be repaired and put back on the road.

The higher levels, the car is damaged beyond repair, the car can be sold but only for parts. It must not end up back on the road. If airbags had gone off, the damage would be major.
