Partner flooded driver's seat

Author: themerger

*000*2024-12-10 21:22

My partner works as a nursing assistant and often has stressful days at work. This particular day had left them 'brain broken' as they like to say. They called me on their way home and unloaded about both their patients and facility leadership. Occasionally they would interject that they really needed to pee. They hadn't gone beforehand because they wanted to leave at the end of their shift rather then feel compelled to stay beyond their scheduled time.

They were also talking about just being exhausted and uncertain whether this job was right for them (they've only been at it about 6-7 months). I was doing my best to listen and help them talk through their stress when suddenly they just said, "oh my god, babe, I really need to go". I told them they could call me back and they responded with, "no, no, I'm actually going". It was then I realized they meant that they were peeing. I asked if they were pulled over and they said that they were already parked in their parking lot just wetting themself in the driver's seat.

I really felt for them as I knew this had been a very stressful day. They repeated how badly they had needed to pee and continued wetting for nearly 30 seconds. They have a newer SUV and I asked if they had a way to clean the seat. They responded that they didn't even have the capacity to think about that and just had to breathe and sit. We talked for another 10 minutes as they collected themself and mustered the energy to go inside. They simply threw a towel down, went inside to change and shower, then drove to my house.

When they arrived they said that they would just deal with the seat the next morning. To our surprise the car didn't smell at all! Not sure if the fabric seats already come pre-treated or something, but they simply let it dry naturally. The seats are also black which helps hide the stain, but the lack of smell left me dumbfounded. I guess they were pretty hydrated!

Luckily they are feeling much more settled into the job after a slight change in position and a more consistent working schedule. Full time benefits are solid too! To be clear- I did get my partner's permission to share this. Naturally, they really weren't fazed by the actual wetting.
